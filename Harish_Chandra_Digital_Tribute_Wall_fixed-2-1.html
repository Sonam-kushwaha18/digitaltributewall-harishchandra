Mathematician · 1923—1983Dr. Harish-Chandra

A life devoted to uncovering the deep structures beneath mathematical forms.

Explore each milestone. The timeline highlights as you move through it.

11 OCTOBER 1923

Beginnings in Kanpur

Harish-Chandra was born in Kanpur, then part of British India. His early education led him toward physics and mathematics.

1940s

From physics to mathematics

After studying at the University of Allahabad, he pursued advanced study in England. His interests increasingly turned toward the mathematical foundations of theoretical physics.

1947–1949

Cambridge and a new direction

At the University of Cambridge, he worked with Paul Dirac. The experience helped shape his transition from physics to the abstract language of mathematics.

1950

Princeton and research

Harish-Chandra joined the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, where he developed a sustained programme of research in representation theory and harmonic analysis.

1950s–1970s

Building a mathematical framework

His work on Lie groups, representations, and harmonic analysis became foundational for later research across mathematics and mathematical physics.

1983 AND BEYOND

A legacy that keeps unfolding

He died in Princeton in 1983. The ideas he developed continue to guide mathematicians exploring symmetry, structure, and representations.
